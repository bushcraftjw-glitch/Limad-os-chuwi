Welcome to LiMaD OS 3.0

LiMaD OS combines Ubuntu 26.04 LTS Desktop FULL with a distinct LiMaD interface, WhiteSur, LiMaD Icons, LiMaD wallpapers and integrated LiMaD tools.

The Ubuntu kernel and driver base is preserved. LiMaD adds design, applications and targeted hardware compatibility, including selected Intel-based Apple systems.
